## STORY

Five teenage friends enter an abandoned amusement park that has been closed for decades.

At first, they believe they are simply exploring an old carnival filled with forgotten rides and abandoned attractions. But after one of them discovers an old golden amusement park ticket, strange things begin happening.

The park appears to be reacting to their presence.

Lights turn on by themselves. Old rides begin moving. Speakers play distorted carnival music. Hidden rooms and forgotten attractions begin revealing themselves.

The deeper the five friends travel into the park, the more they realize that the amusement park was abandoned for a reason.

And the golden ticket may have been waiting for them.
